Common Questions

Do I need my own video or graphics?

No. Choose "generate for me" during setup, describe your product, and our team builds it. You approve it before it's shown to anyone.

How do I pay?

Instantly, by mobile money or card, in whichever of our seven supported currencies you choose. There's no wallet top-up — you pay per campaign.

Who actually sees my ad?

Real people on our OrbitEarn network who've verified a TikTok, YouTube, or X account matching your business category and target location.
